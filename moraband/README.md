## Integrity / attribution

- The generated RSS is an **unofficial listener-selected index**, not a new audio release. It links directly to the publisher's original MP3s at `archive.org/download/heroes-s-3-a-3-e-N/HeroesS3A3EN.mp3`; no recordings were downloaded, altered or rehosted.
- MP3 names and positive exact byte sizes for **all 11** were checked against the Internet Archive metadata API, and episode titles and article permalinks against the publisher's archive on **2026-11-14**.
- Episode GUIDs are stable, unique curation identifiers. The RSS item `pubDate` values are **fixed artificial sort markers** making episode 10 appear before episode 11 in newest-first podcast players; the actual 2022 publisher posting dates appear in each description. The synthetic markers must not be presented as 2026 recording dates.
- Episodes 1–9 and the post-arc Q&A episodes are intentionally excluded. Episode 10 is included as the direct lead-in; episode 20 completes the aftermath.
- Original recording rights remain with the creators. No rights in the podcast audio are claimed by this index.
