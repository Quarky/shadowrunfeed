# Shadowrun Arcology Feed

A filtered RSS feed containing only the **SINless** episodes in the Renraku Arcology mini-arc.

## Subscribe

`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Quarky/shadowrunfeed/main/feed.xml`

Paste that URL into a podcast app that supports **Add by RSS URL / Add podcast by URL**.

## Included episodes

- Season 2 Episode 20 — Kami-Kaze (Arcology setup/prologue)
- Season 2 Episode 22 — Blood, Sweat & Stairs
- Season 2 Episode 23 — Directory Resistance
- Season 2 Episode 24 — Oh Bee-Have
- Season 2 Episode 25 — Traumatic Irony
- Season 2 Episode 26 — Emergency Boom

Season 2 Episode 21 is intentionally omitted because it leaves the Arcology storyline.

The feed is generated from the show's official RSS feed, so audio remains hosted by the original publisher and the original episode metadata/enclosures are preserved.
